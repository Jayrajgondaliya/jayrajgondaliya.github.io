Surrender Isn’t Weakness — This Book Proved It

Surrender. What does this word really mean, and why am I even talking about it? Most people associate surrender with weakness. But is surrender truly a weakness, or can it lead us to contentment, inner peace, and something greater? Michael A. Singer wrote this book and shared his experiences with surrender. But surrender to what? It is surrendering to the will of the universe. It means accepting situations and circumstances as they are, without adding our personal preferences or trying to remove what we dislike, while staying detached from the outcome. Instead, surrender is about letting go of resistance and focusing on serving whatever life places in front of us, to the best of our ability. ...
