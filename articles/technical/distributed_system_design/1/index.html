Building the Internet #1: The New Rivers

June 1, 2026 · Jayraj Gondaliya

Thousands of years ago, the world’s greatest cities were built near rivers.

The Nile gave birth to civilizations in Egypt. The Indus River supported some of the earliest cities in human history. Rivers provided water, transportation, food, and trade.

Later, rivers became less important than roads and railways. Cities that connected people and goods more efficiently grew faster.

Today, a similar shift is happening.

Modern digital cities are built around a different kind of infrastructure: fiber-optic cables, data centers, and global networks.

That’s why, we are seeing big techs are more involved in the growth of new cities and countries.

Have you ever wondered - How this big Systems like Instagram(Social Media), Youtube, linkedIn, Google, etc. works ?

They are powered by vast collections of computers(Millions) working together across the globe to achieve that phenomenal experiences that we enjoy everyday.

Secret lies in ‘Distributed systems’

If you’re anything like me, you’ve probably heard the term many times.

I certainly had, But when I started preparing for engineering roles at larger technology companies, like FAANG.
